2. Physical Governing Equations & Analytical Formulations

The simulation engine integrates continuous incompressible Navier–Stokes momentum conservation with hydrostatic pressure gradients, thermodynamic fluid properties, and analytical solid-boundary masking:

∇ · u = 0 [Mass Incompressibility]
∂u∂t + (u · ∇)u = −1ρ∇p + ν∇2u + g + gβ(T − Tamb) [Momentum with Hydrostatic & Buoyant Forcing]

Ocean Hydrodynamic Pressure Gradient & Cavitation Inception: For marine and underwater archetypes (e.g., submarine hull, submerged hydrofoils), the fluid medium is modeled with high-salinity seawater parameters (ρ = 1025.0 kg/m3), where the hydrostatic pressure field p(z) and cavitation index σ are evaluated as:

Δphydro = ρ · g · h,      σ = p∞ − pvap½ ρ U∞2 [Hydrostatic Gradient & Cavitation Index]

where h is submerged water depth, pvap ≈ 2338 Pa is the saturation vapor pressure of water at 20°C, and cavitation onset is flagged whenever σ ≤ 1.0.

Parametric NACA 4-Digit Airfoil Geometry Formulation: To simulate arbitrary wings and lifting surfaces without external CAD meshes, the obstacle mask evaluates chord-normalized coordinates (x/c) rotated through angle of attack α:

yt = 0.6c · [0.2969√(x/c) − 0.1260(x/c) − 0.3516(x/c)2 + 0.2843(x/c)3 − 0.1015(x/c)4] [NACA 4-Digit Thickness Distribution]
x' = (x − cx)cos(α) + (y − cy)sin(α),      y' = −(x − cx)sin(α) + (y − cy)cos(α) [Coordinate Centroid Rotation]

Live Dynamic Surface Force Integration: Rather than relying on empirical correlations, the PINN AI surrogate and numerical solver execute discrete surface integrals of local pressure and viscous shear stress over the immersed boundary ∂Ωsolid:

Ftotal = ∮∂Ωsolid [ −p n + μ(∇u + (∇u)T)n ] dA [Surface Stress Integral]
C_d = Fx½ ρ U∞2 A,      C_l = Fy½ ρ U∞2 A,      Fdown = ½ ρ U∞2 A |C_l| [Non-Dimensional Telemetry Coefficients]
