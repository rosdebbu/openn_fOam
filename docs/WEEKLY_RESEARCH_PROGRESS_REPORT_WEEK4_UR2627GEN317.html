2. Physical Governing Equations & Numerical Formulations

The expanded simulation engine discretizes continuous Navier–Stokes conservation coupled with natural gravity body forces, thermal buoyancy, and immersed solid obstacle boundaries:

∇ · u = 0 [Mass Incompressibility]
∂u∂t + (u · ∇)u = −1ρ∇p + ν∇2u + g + gβ(T − Tamb) [Momentum with Gravity & Thermal Buoyancy]

where u = (u, v, w) is the velocity field, p is kinematic pressure, ν is kinematic viscosity, ρ is fluid density, g = (0, 0, −9.81 m/s2) is gravitational acceleration, and β = 1/Tamb is the thermal expansion coefficient.

∂C∂t + ∇ · (C u) = 0     ρeff = C ρliquid + (1 − C) ρgas [Volume-of-Fluid (VOF) Phase Tracking]

Multi-Archetype Aerodynamic Force Formulations: Aerodynamic forces and vehicle-specific telemetry are computed dynamically across each archetype:

C_l = 2π sin(α) × 0.88,    C_d = 0.042 + C_l2π · AR + 0.015|α| + ΔCd,wake [NACA Airfoil Lift & Induced Drag]
Fdown = ½ρ U2 A |C_l|      σ = p∞ − pvap½ρ U2 [F1 Downforce & Submarine Cavitation Index]

Fractional-Step Projection Scheme: Advective fluxes are computed via upwind differencing and viscous diffusion via 2nd-order central differences, followed by solving the elliptic Pressure-Poisson equation using pre-conditioned banded SciPy CSR matrices:

∇2p = ρΔt ∇ · u*   ⇒   un+1 = u* − Δtρ ∇p [Poisson Solve & Divergence-Free Projection]
